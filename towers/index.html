Chapter 1 Preview

The Disappearance

“Jesus, Laura, will you shut up for once!” Johnno stopped abruptly and swung round to face her. The tape recorder in her hand went dead with a click. For a moment, there was only the soft rustling of the paperbark trees above them. “You’re not FBI Special Agent Dale Cooper and this isn’t Twin Peaks. This is real life. If Ty Senior catches us following him, we’re going to be in serious trouble.” The moonlight caught Johnno’s face between the branches; he looked wide-eyed and pale, his red hair damp against his forehead.

Laura lowered the tape recorder. “Well, you’re certainly doing a lot of talking for someone who’s telling me to shut up.” She narrowed her eyes and smiled at him. “Now, if you don’t mind, instead of yelling at me for doing my job, next time you’re afraid, just tell me—and I’ll give you a little cuddle and we can get back to solving this case.”

Johnno looked down, pawing at the dirt with his boot. “I’m not afraid, all right.”

But she was already gone, ducking beneath the low branches and moving towards the water. By the time he caught up, she had raised the recorder to her lips again. “Detective Laura Harris resuming her report. It’s nearly midnight on the first of September 1994—conditions on the southern edge of the Dawesville Estuary are clear, approximately twenty degrees, with a full moon providing excellent visibility. We are currently pursuing the suspect, Mr Daniel Ty—”

“Oh my God, will you at least whisper?” he hissed.

She lowered her voice. “—who is approximately one hundred metres offshore and appears to be operating some kind of fishing device.”
