A glimpse inside Towers:

Dawesville is the second-last town before Perth, the world’s most isolated major city. But in the spring of 1994, on the banks of the 52 square mile Dawesville Estuary, things are changing.

Twins Laura and Johnno Harris, fifteen, have noticed it, but it seems like they’re the only ones: fish and crabs dying without explanation, toxic algae blooms, taps that won’t run, sick seabirds. Something is wrong. Then people start disappearing.

When Mary Macy’s twelve-year-old sister goes missing during the annual Prince of Peel sailing regatta, the twins are left with no choice: embrace the darkness and descend into a world of lake monsters, tipping ecosystems, and corrupt townspeople.

With the help of an unlikely gang—rural it girl Mary Macy, town outcast Kayden Ty, and Noah Peel, the youngest son of the mayor—they begin uncovering the truth behind the disappearances.

The trail leads to ex-mayor Thomas Peel’s sprawling family dynasty, illegal land clearing, poisoned water, and a terrifying creature forced up from beneath the estuary in search of clean water: Grendel.

To save those still missing, Laura and Johnno must confront not only the supernatural terror in the water, but the families who profited while the ecosystem collapsed.
