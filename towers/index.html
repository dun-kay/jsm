Dawesville is the second-last town before Perth, the most isolated major city in the world.

In 1994, on the edge of the estuary, the old certainties are beginning to rot. Fish die. Crabs wash up. Seabirds fall sick. The water tastes wrong. Everyone notices. Almost nobody says it out loud.

Laura and Johnno Harris are fifteen, twins, and old enough to know when adults are lying badly.

When a twelve-year-old girl goes missing during the Prince of Peel sailing regatta, the twins stop watching from the edge of town and start pulling at the thing everyone else wants buried.

The trail leads through old families, cleared land, poisoned water, and the town’s distinctive towers.

Something has been forced up from beneath the estuary. Something hungry. Something older than the town pretending it owns the place.
