A Note On AI Writing

I write my stories.

I don't use AI to generate original concepts, create story arcs, outline entire stories, or write episodes or prose for me. The characters, worlds, plots and words you are reading are mine.

I do use AI as an editing and research tool. I use it to check spelling, grammar, continuity, tone, point of view and flow; to question things that aren't working; and to catch mistakes I might otherwise miss. I review every suggestion myself and decide what belongs in the final work.

I also use AI much as I've always used Google: to research places, dates, history and other factual details, and to challenge my assumptions so I can make the world of the story as truthful as possible.

AI has made this process dramatically faster for me. Pretending I don't use it would be dishonest, and refusing to use it would make it much harder for me to produce these stories at the pace I want to.

If that isn't how you believe writers should work, I completely respect your decision not to purchase my work.

And if you write, draw, make films, games, music, comics -- or create stories in any form -- I respect the hell out of you. However you make them, keep making them.
