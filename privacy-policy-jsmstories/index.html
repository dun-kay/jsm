The site may use cookies, local storage, pixels and similar technologies for login sessions, reader preferences, analytics, advertising measurement and fraud prevention. Google explains how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services at google.com/policies/privacy/partners.

Information may be used to sign you in, unlock paid episodes, process payments, send release notifications, send marketing where permitted, provide support, prevent abuse, improve the site and measure advertising performance.

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the site, including the services listed above. Payment card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored by JSM Stories.
